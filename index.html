Automated Diploid Genotype Screening

Upload a multi-locus microsatellite or SNP population dataset (.csv) to evaluate observed heterozygosity (Ho), expected heterozygosity (He), and inbreeding coefficient (Fis). Calculations detect potential genetic bottleneck screening signals and heterozygosity deficits across loci.

Dataset Ingestion

Diploid genotypes formatted with paired locus columns (Locus1_A1, Locus1_A2).

Max size: 4 MB
Click to choose file or drag and drop

Supported format: Standard CSV (.csv) with paired diploid allele columns

View expected column structure & validation rules ▼

Example Valid CSV Layout:

Individual_ID,Locus1_A1,Locus1_A2,Locus2_A1,Locus2_A2,Locus3_A1,Locus3_A2
Ind_01,142,150,104,104,210,214
Ind_02,142,142,100,104,210,210
Ind_03,150,150,104,108,NA,NA
  • Allele columns must follow Locus<N>_A1 and Locus<N>_A2 paired naming.
  • Metadata columns like Individual_ID are automatically ignored during calculations.
  • Missing allele calls are omitted pairwise per locus. Numeric allele codes are treated strictly as categories.
